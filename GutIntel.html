02 · Our Insight

Location is the signal.

Sequencing tells you the cast of characters. Spatial omics tells you who is touching whom — and that's where mechanism lives.

Method A · Sequencing

What 16S and metagenomics see.

A roster of species and their abundance — homogenized, stripped of location. The industry's status quo for 20 years.

Method B · Spatial Omics + AI

What GutIntel sees.

A spatial map: which strain is co-localized with which neuron, EC cell, or immune cell — at single-cell resolution.
